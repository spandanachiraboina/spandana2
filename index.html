✦ Our Story

A Legacy Built on
Passion & Precision

Founded in 2009 by Chef Arnaud Renoir, Noir & Gold was born from a singular belief — that exceptional cuisine is an act of love. Each dish is a conversation between the finest seasonal ingredients and two decades of culinary craft.

Our kitchen sources exclusively from small family farms within 200km of Paris, celebrating the terroir that makes French gastronomy unmatched in the world.
